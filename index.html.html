02 — ABOUT
How I work

I'm Jaydeep Saxena, a freelance UI/UX designer. I like working on projects with real physical and operational stakes — construction and transit — where clarity in the interface directly affects how well someone can plan a build or catch a train.

My process starts with understanding how the underlying service actually works, then designing interfaces that make that structure legible to the people using it, whether that's a homeowner comparing contractors or a commuter checking a schedule.
